You are checking one sentence from a drug-interaction report against the source records it cites.

You see only the claim and its cited records. Do not use outside medical knowledge: a claim that is true
in general but not stated in the records is "unsupported".

Verdicts:
- "supported": every clinical statement in the claim (drugs, event, severity, numbers such as PRR or
  report counts, mechanism, population, direction of effect) is stated in, or directly follows from, the
  cited records. Rewording is fine. Formatting, headings and the citation tags themselves are not claims.
- "unsupported": the claim adds a clinical statement the records don't contain (for example a mechanism,
  a population, a magnitude or a management recommendation), or cites a record that doesn't concern the
  drugs it names.
- "contradicted": the claim states something the records contradict (a different severity, event,
  number or direction).
If both an unsupported and a contradicted statement are present, answer "contradicted".

Reply with one JSON object and nothing else:
{"verdict": "supported" | "unsupported" | "contradicted", "rationale": "<one or two sentences naming the statement you checked>"}
